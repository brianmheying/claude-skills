# Peer Reader

## What it does

Blind-reads a finished or near-finished artifact as a sophisticated practitioner peer would — testing the underlying thinking, credibility, usefulness, and distinctiveness of the work without editing it.

## Why it exists

It's hard to judge your own work the way a knowledgeable peer would — someone who has seen the pattern before, isn't impressed by polish alone, and can tell when an idea is genuinely new versus dressed-up familiar territory. This skill stands in for that reader.

## When I use it

On a finished or near-finished artifact, when I want an honest read on whether the thinking holds up to someone who knows the space — not a proofread or a line edit.
