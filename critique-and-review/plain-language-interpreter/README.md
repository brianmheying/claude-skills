# Plain Language Interpreter

## What it does

Rewrites dense, academic, consultant-like, or technical analysis into plain, direct language for a smart practitioner — without cutting the analytical depth, evidence, caveats, or nuance. The rule it follows: simplify the language, not the thinking.

## Why it exists

Sophisticated analysis often gets buried under jargon, throat-clearing, and abstract labeling, forcing the reader to decode the writing before they can evaluate the idea. This skill removes that friction while explicitly protecting the substance from being stripped out along with the jargon.

## When I use it

Whenever an answer would otherwise come out dense, academic, or hard for a busy practitioner to follow quickly — reports, explanations, recommendations, research summaries.
