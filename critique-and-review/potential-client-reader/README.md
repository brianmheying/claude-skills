# Potential Client Reader

## What it does

Blind-reads an artifact as a skeptical B2B executive who could hire the author — testing commercial trust, usefulness, execution credibility, and genuine buying demand, without editing the artifact.

## Why it exists

Writing that reads well to its author doesn't always read as credible to someone deciding whether to spend money on them. This skill simulates that harder, more skeptical read before the artifact goes in front of a real prospective client.

## When I use it

On a finished or near-finished artifact aimed at a potential client or buyer, when I want to know whether it would actually generate commercial trust and demand, not just whether it reads well.
